À propos du club

Who we are

A vibrant community of French language enthusiasts at TUK, united by a passion for francophone culture, language, and connection.

The TUK French Club — Le Club Français — was founded to create a space where students from all academic disciplines can explore the French language and the rich tapestry of francophone cultures around the world.

Whether you're just starting out with bonjour or you're already conversational, our club welcomes you. We run weekly sessions, cultural events, film screenings, conversation circles, and our own podcast — all in the heart of Nairobi.

French is spoken by over 300 million people across 5 continents. Learning it opens doors to international careers, cultural exchange, and academic opportunities in the Francophone world — including right here in East Africa.

Join us

Become part of Le Club Français — all students at TUK are welcome, regardless of French level.

Why join Le Club Français?

Membership is open to all TUK students. There's no minimum level — just curiosity and a willingness to learn. Here's what you get:

  • Weekly conversation circles and language workshops
  • Access to exclusive events — Bastille Day, Ciné-club, cultural weeks
  • Opportunity to be part of our podcast production team
  • DELF/DALF exam prep support and guidance
  • Network with French-speaking professionals and alumni
  • Certificate of membership for your CV/portfolio
